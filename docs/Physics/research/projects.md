# Projects

## SEPnet Competitive Funded Summer Research Programme — [CQER-IQ](https://cqer-iq.com/)
**Jun 2026 – Aug 2026.** Project co-supervisors:
[Dr. Richard Howl](https://pure.royalholloway.ac.uk/en/persons/richard-howl), Prof. Sougato Bose,
Dr. Sravan Kumar.

- Investigated entanglement measures in realistic scenario for an experimental proposal utilising
  Bose-Einstein Condensates, and their robustness under realistic noise models in support of ongoing
  research into tests of Quantum and Modified Gravity.
- Derived analytical solutions of entanglement witnesses and mathematical proofs.
- Implemented numerical simulations utilising Lindblad Master Equations, Stochastic Differential
  Equations, and Monte Carlo methods.

## Ongoing Research Projects — Royal Holloway
**2025–present.** Independent research, continued from previous summer projects.

- Developed Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) approaches for solving nonlinear PDEs and created
  computational notebooks and a Python package for the Schrödinger-Newton equation
  ([:fontawesome-brands-github: Learning-PINN](https://github.com/conradkhho/Learning-PINN/)).
- Investigated tensions between tests of quantum gravity and gravitationally-induced collapse models, and analysed their mutual implications.

## Casimir Effect in Tabletop Experiment for Quantum Gravity
**Royal Holloway, Aug 2025.** Project supervisor: Dr. Richard Howl.

- Investigated the effects of gravitational redshift on the Casimir contribution in the
  Bose–Marletto–Vedral experiment.
- Performed von Neumann entropy calculations and computational simulations using Wolfram Mathematica
  ([:fontawesome-brands-github: Gravity-Entropy](https://github.com/conradkhho/Gravity-Entropy)).
