# Tutoring

> Private online tutor for Mathematics and Physics since Jul 2025

Most tutoring aims only at the exam, which teaches you nothing about how to think. My tutorials bridge the gap from school-level work to thinking like a researcher.

**Levels:** GCSE · A Level · First/Second-year undergraduate    
**Format:** Online  
**Language:** English, Cantonese 

## My approach

Lessons are modelled on university tutorials. Student attempt the problem first, then we investigate where the reasoning fails, just like a researcher. The goal is a problem-solving toolkit that works on unseen problems in exams and beyond in life.

I work best with ambitious students: GCSE and A Level students aiming for top grades or competitive courses, and undergraduates finding the step up harder than expected.

**These tutorials involve real work, but anyone with genuine intention will not be disappointed.**

## How we can work together

- **Customised weekly tutorial sheets:** problem sets built around your weak areas, updated as you improve.
- **Lecture-style revision sessions:** for topics you didn't follow in lessons, tailored to your needs.
- **Live past paper sessions:** you work through a paper in the session, then I give feedback, exam technique and work through misconceptions.
- **Admissions test and interview preparation:** test practice, interview-style problems and feedback on your approach.
- **Message support** Every plan includes up to 5 written questions a month, with a reply within 24 hours on weekdays. Send your question with your own attempt attached. Unused questions don't carry over. A premium option with more questions and faster replies is available on request.

Most students combine these, and we adjust the mix over time.
