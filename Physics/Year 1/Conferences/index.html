Conferences

During my 1st year, I attended two undergraduate conferences including the QUIRK conference and CuWiP+ conference held in Imperial College London and University of Warwick respectively.

QUIRK Conference

QUIRK Conference is an undergraduate physics conference held by 5 institutions' Physics societies including:

  • Queen Mary University of London (QMUL)
  • University College London (UCL)
  • Imperial College London (ICL)
  • Royal Holloway University of London (RHUL)
  • Kings College London (KCL)

During the conference, I also made a keynote presentation about Fractional Dynamics — a similar presentation can be found with my PH1150 Talk.

CuWiP+

CuWiP+ stands for Conference for Undergraduate Women and Non-Binary People in Physics. During this conference held in Warwick, we had opportunities to socialise with fellow WiP+, attend keynote presentations, etc. — I also had the pleasure of meeting Dame Jocelyn Bell Burnell!
