Research Interests

I am in interested in the Foundation of Qunatum Mechanics (or Quantum Foundation, QF for short!). In particularly, I am interested in:

  • Tests on Non-Classicality: such as a proposed experiment by Bose et. al., as well as, Marletto and Vedral which tests the quantum nature of gravity.
  • Collapse Models

...as well as the intersection between them, and application of Quantum Information Theory to give us insights about some fundamental, yet remaining open, questions in Quantum Theory.
